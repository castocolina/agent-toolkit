# Notes: "Fix Your Checkout" (CRO course, module 3)

## 3.1 Talk before you test
- Ask 5 recent customers where checkout felt slow or confusing. Write down their exact words.
- Use their words, not yours, in the next copy change.

## 3.2 One change at a time
- Ship ONE copy change on the checkout page (button, shipping note or error text).
- Compare checkout conversion for a week before and after. Small shops: expect noise.

## 3.3 Mindset
- "The best founders never stop testing."
- "Every great store was built one experiment at a time."

## 3.4 Later
- Once a copy change wins, try removing one form field.
