# Pocketbrew

Pocketbrew is your tiny, caffeinated sidekick that lives in your terminal and absolutely, positively refuses to let you run out of coffee beans ever again. Seriously. We mean it.

## Getting started

First things first: grab yourself a mug, because it is time to install Pocketbrew! Just run `brew install pocketbrew` and, before you know it, you will be well on your way to a world of perfectly timed bean reorders that basically take care of themselves.

## Syncing your pantry

Pocketbrew keeps an eye on your pantry for you. To see what it would reorder without actually placing any orders at all, you can go ahead and run `pocketbrew sync --dry-run`, which is a totally safe way to peek behind the curtain. When you are happy with what you see, drop the flag and let the magic happen.
