## How Fernwood Logistics cut onboarding time

It is worth mentioning that Fernwood Logistics, which is a regional freight company based in Leeds, made the decision to move all of its driver onboarding paperwork over to Formline at the start of the 2025 financial year, and in doing so it was able to reduce the time that it takes to onboard a new driver from nine working days down to three working days.

Their operations lead, Priya Nair, put it this way: "Honestly, before Formline we were basically drowning in paper, and now we are not drowning in paper anymore, which is really, really great for us."

At the present time, Fernwood is in the process of rolling Formline out to its warehouse staff as well.
